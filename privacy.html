Privacy Policy

What this static storefront uses, what Paddle handles, and how to reach support.

Last updated: 9 October 2026 · Review business details before launch.

01

What this site does

WEB CLIENT OS is a static storefront for a digital ebook. When configured, it uses Paddle.js to show localized prices and open Paddle Checkout. This site does not run its own account system or process card details directly. Paddle handles checkout and payment processing under its own policies.

02

Information you provide

This site has no account registration or contact form. If you contact support by email, phone, or WhatsApp, the information you choose to share is processed by those services and by the support operator. Complete before launch: [LEGAL CONTROLLER NAME AND BUSINESS ADDRESS].

03

Localized pricing

When configured, the website requests a localized price preview from Paddle based on visitor location. If unavailable, it falls back to the ₹199 INR base price. Final currency, amount, and applicable taxes are confirmed in checkout.

04

Cookies and third parties

This implementation does not add an analytics SDK or advertising cookies. Paddle.js may use cookies or similar technologies and process device, browser, location, and transaction details to operate checkout. Read Paddle’s privacy notice. Third party services may update their practices independently.

05

Retention, rights, and contact

This static website does not create a durable purchase database or verify order entitlements. Complete the seller’s retention schedule and applicable privacy rights process here: [RETENTION SCHEDULE AND PRIVACY CONTACT]. For support, email dewifystores@gmail.com.
